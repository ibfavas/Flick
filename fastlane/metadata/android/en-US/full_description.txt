Draw a gesture to open an app, visit a webpage, call a contact or run a phone action. Flick recognises gestures on your device.

Tap a home screen widget or the Quick Settings tile to open the drawing pad. Choose a full-screen pad or a floating card. A successful match shows the target before it opens. A missed match gives you one retry.

Record up to three samples per gesture. Flick warns about similar shapes, learns from successful uses and lets you adjust match strictness. Check usage counts in Stats and recent launches in History. Long-press a gesture to edit, test, duplicate or delete it.

Phone actions include flashlight control, camera, media playback, screen lock, screenshots, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth settings, and Do Not Disturb. Contact-call gestures support custom photos.

One accent colours your strokes, widgets and controls. Choose from eight presets, including Sapphire, Copper and Champagne Gold. Back up your library with Android backup or export and import a JSON file.

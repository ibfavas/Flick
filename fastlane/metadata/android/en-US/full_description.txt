Flick is a fully local Android gesture launcher. Draw a gesture to launch apps, open links, or run phone actions. No backend, no accounts, no data leaves your phone.

Draw on the invisible home screen widget or the Quick Settings tile to open the transparent drawing pad, then draw your gesture and the matched action runs.

Features:
- Gesture pad with a matcher written from scratch: a multi-stroke recognizer blended with banded DTW. Sloppy, tilted and reversed strokes still match, and near misses do not launch the wrong thing. No ML model, no network.
- Gesture library: record gestures, name them, attach actions, edit, delete, enable or disable, and test them. Up to 3 samples per gesture. Everything is stored locally in a SQLite database. Backup and restore as JSON.
- Actions: launch an app, open a URL, call a contact, lock the screen, take a screenshot, toggle the flashlight, open the camera, play or pause media, open Wi-Fi or Bluetooth settings, toggle Do Not Disturb.
- Flows: chain several actions into one gesture.
- Invisible widget: a fully transparent home screen widget in round and wide styles, plus a Quick Settings tile.
- Themes: AMOLED black with a choice of accent colour, adjustable stroke colour and width.
